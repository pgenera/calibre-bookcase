A bookcase for a calibre library

This is a demo with 151 books old enough to be in the public domain. Pointed at a calibre server, it shows that library the same way.

  • Drag sideways to turn the shelves, and up or down to move along them.
  • Pinch, or hold Ctrl and scroll, to zoom.
  • Tap a book to take it off the shelf.

Use it with your own library
